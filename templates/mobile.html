📐 How to Position Your Phone:
  1. Mount or hold your phone facing the store doorway or hallway.
  2. Align the red dashed tripwire line across the walking threshold.
  3. People walking downward across the line will be counted as Entered.
  4. People walking upward across the line will be counted as Exited.
🔒 Camera Blocked by Mobile Browser?

Mobile Chrome/Safari restricts camera access on local IP addresses unless allowed:

1. Open Chrome on this phone & go to: chrome://flags
2. Search: unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure
3. Set to Enabled and enter: http://{{ local_ip }}:5000
4. Tap Relaunch Chrome. Done!

Alternate 1-Minute Method: Install the free IP Webcam app (Android) or DroidCam, start server, and enter the phone's URL on the PC dashboard!
